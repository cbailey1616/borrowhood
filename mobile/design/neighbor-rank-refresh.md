- **Jester:** existing sage and amber cap.
- **Archer:** curved recurve bow with a drawn arrow, sage fill, and muted brass details.

The shared drawings update rank badges beside names as well as the rating details sheet. The meter progresses through dusty red, amber, olive, sage, and forest green. The existing rank thresholds and three-exchange new-neighbor state are unchanged. The decorative tree has its own icon name for neighborhood artwork and environmental statistics.

Validation: the existing icon, member-summary, reputation, and sustainability suites passed (40 tests). Artwork was inspected at 20, 24, and 28 pt; all 118 existing SVGs parsed, and the other drawings were compared with the previous version.
