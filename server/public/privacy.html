Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Borrowhood ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

We collect information you provide directly, including:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, hashed password, profile photo, and email verification status
  • Identity Verification: Stripe handles the ID images and selfie submitted for identity verification. Borrowhood receives verification results and identity details; ID images are not shown to neighbors.
  • Profile Information: Bio, neighborhood, profile preferences
  • Listing Information: Item descriptions, photos, pricing, availability
  • Transaction Information: Borrow requests, pickup and return confirmations, completed exchanges, and private messages including photos
  • Payment Information: Borrowhood does not collect or process payments for current item exchanges. Any fee is arranged directly between neighbors. Historical payment records may remain from earlier payment features.
  • Communications: Messages to other users, support requests, feedback

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Service, we automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Location Information: Town or neighborhood you choose. With permission, the app can use device location to suggest your town; that onboarding flow sends your town and state, not precise device coordinates, to Borrowhood
  • Usage Information: Features used, pages viewed, actions taken, time spent
  • Log Information: Access times, app crashes, system activity

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from:

  • Identity verification services
  • Payment processors
  • Social media platforms (if you link accounts)
  • Other users (messages, replies, reports, and exchange confirmations)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

Purpose Examples
Provide the Service Create accounts, process transactions, enable messaging
Safety and Security Verify identity, prevent fraud, enforce policies
Personalization Show relevant listings, customize recommendations
Communication Send notifications, updates, support responses
Improvement Analyze usage, fix bugs, develop new features
Legal Compliance Comply with laws, respond to legal requests

3. How We Share Your Information

3.1 With Other Users

We share certain information with other users to facilitate transactions:

  • Profile information made available through the Service, including name, photo, completed exchanges, and identity verification status
  • Listing information within the audience you select: friends, neighborhood, or town. Items kept private stay in your inventory. A private offer shares the selected item with the requester.
  • Private messages and chat photos with the conversation participants. Public replies are visible to people who can see the original post.
  • Approximate location (neighborhood level, not exact address)

3.2 With Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who help us operate the Service:

  • Cloud hosting providers
  • Payment processors
  • Identity verification services
  • Analytics providers
  • Customer support tools
  • Push notification services

3.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose information when required by law or to:

  • Comply with legal process
  • Protect rights, property, or safety
  • Investigate potential violations
  • Prevent fraud or illegal activity

3.4 Business Transfers

If Borrowhood is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

3.5 With Your Consent

We may share information with third parties when you give us permission to do so.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as:

  • Your account is active
  • Needed to provide the Service
  • Required by law
  • Necessary for legitimate business purposes

After account deletion, we may retain certain information for legal compliance, dispute resolution, and fraud prevention for up to 7 years.

5. Your Rights and Choices

5.1 Account Information

You can update your account in Profile. To delete your account, open Profile and choose Delete Account. For help or a privacy request, contact chris@borrowhood.net.

5.2 Location Information

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location may limit certain features.

5.3 Push Notifications

You can manage notification preferences in the app or through device settings.

5.4 Marketing Communications

For questions about marketing communications or to opt out, contact chris@borrowhood.net. In-app notification settings control the notification categories shown there.

5.5 Data Portability

You can request a copy of your data by contacting chris@borrowhood.net.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights:

  • Right to Know: Request information about data collected about you
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Equal service regardless of exercising privacy rights

To exercise these rights, contact chris@borrowhood.net.

7. International Users (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), UK, or Switzerland, you have additional rights:

  • Access: Request access to your personal data
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your data
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing
  • Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to certain processing activities

Our legal bases for processing include contract performance, legitimate interests, and consent.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Regular security assessments
  • Employee access controls

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

Borrowhood is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we learn we have collected information from a child, we will delete it promptly.
